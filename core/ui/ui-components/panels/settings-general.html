DSP

Preset Switching

Lets the preset you just left keep ringing out over the new one. Its input fades away, and its delay and reverb state is kept for the selected number of bars before fading out. Bars are counted at the current tempo, and each preset still ringing costs CPU. Switching again may end an older tail sooner.

A centred rig sits 3 dB below its input, so that it keeps its loudness when panned across a stereo output. A mono output, such as a mono DAW track or a single interface output, has nothing to pan across: this brings the rig back to its input level there. Stereo outputs are unchanged. In a DAW it applies to instances you add after turning it on, and saved projects keep the level they were made with.
